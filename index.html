person Summary

Hi! I'm Max, a Senior Level Designer and Level Artist specializing in full-cycle environment production — from a blank canvas and initial gameplay concept to a polished, optimized, shippable game space.

I bridge the gap between gameplay and visuals, combining level design, environment art, composition, lighting, and optimization to create engaging and visually cohesive game worlds. I am comfortable owning the entire production process independently: researching references, developing layouts and blockouts, building terrain, dressing environments, setting up prefabs, coordinating asset needs, and taking locations through final polish and release.

As the studio's only Level Designer and Level Artist, I have independently owned level production across its game portfolio, while collaborating closely with Art Direction, Game Design, Tech Art, and 3D artists. On Amikin Survival, I rebuilt the project's original locations and created its entire set of major environments from scratch, while working with Tech Art to overhaul the level production pipeline and significantly improve iteration speed, autonomy, and visual quality.

I am highly autonomous and adaptable, with a strong focus on production efficiency, visual quality, and practical problem-solving. I enjoy turning ambiguous or constrained ideas into finished game content, whether that means building large-scale environments under strict mobile performance budgets, creating complete level art from existing assets, or developing new procedural and AI-assisted workflows for rapid prototyping.
